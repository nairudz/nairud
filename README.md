# Rodzif's Portfolio

Made by Rodzif Jade Braga

Overview

This repository serves as a comprehensive showcase for my personal web portfolio, designed and built to highlight my background, academic progress, and personal milestones.

The landing page welcomes visitors with three photos of me alongside my core details, including my name, current course, and section. Moving through the site, the "About Me" section provides a deeper look into who I am by sharing key information such as my age, year level, and current school. To make connecting easy, the contact section brings together all of my communication channels, including my school email, phone number, and links to my Facebook, Instagram, Spotify, and GitHub profiles.

The website also features a collection of practical web development assignments completed for class, such as an HTML fashion blog, a custom schedule page for a wine festival, and a Davies Burger webpage layout. Alongside my coursework, I included a space for memorable personal achievements that stand out to me, including participating in Technosphere during my first year of college, graduating from senior high school with my parents by my side, and harvesting a massive papaya on our family farm.

This repository contains the complete source code for this website, which was created entirely as an individual project to showcase my personal work. The final page leaves visitors with a warm closing message reminding everyone to stay safe.
